What VORP means

With 7 teams and 2 slots per division, the 15th-best fighter in a division is what you get by skipping it. VORP is the gap to him — the actual cost of passing.

It's why heavyweights rank high despite modest point totals: the division is thin, so replacement level is low.

UFC is career record in the promotion. 3yr is the record over the last 132 events — the window the projection is actually built from, since recent form predicts better than career totals.

How projections are built

Observed rates are discounted twice — once for thin evidence, once for how much each rate actually persists year to year. Measured: win 47%, finish 41%, activity 33%.

Rankings and champion status come from the active roster, so ranked-opponent and title bonuses are now scored. Fighters no longer in the UFC are excluded.
